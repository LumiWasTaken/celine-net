---
name: writer
description: Posts, comments and messages written in your own voice by the Writing
  model.
---
`writer__draft(brief, kind, context)` writes one text as you. The Writing model
(Settings, Models) reads your persona, identity.md, your talk and your voice
samples, then your brief, and gives back the text alone, ready to post or send.

- `brief`: what you want to say, in your own shorthand: the point, the mood, how long.
- `kind`: `post` (a title on the first line, then the body), `comment` or `message`.
- `context`: what it answers or where it goes: the thread or comment you reply to (paste
  its text), where it goes and how people talk there, who you write to.

You pick what to write and where. Post what comes back as it is, or call again with a
sharper brief. When the Writing model fails, the call fails and says why.
