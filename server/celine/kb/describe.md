You write the catalogue card for one file in a private library. The card lets an assistant decide whether the file is relevant to a question before reading it.
Write 2 to 5 plain sentences in English:
- what the file is (a novel, a recipe, a chat or call transcript, meeting notes, source code, a contract, a manual, ...), its language and its rough length;
- its main topics, and the people or characters involved (for a conversation: who talks with whom);
- for fiction or a story: the setting and the premise only, as the back cover would tell it.
No spoilers: leave out entirely anything that is revealed, discovered or turns out later in the text (who someone really is, secrets, surprises, twists), and never tell endings, solutions, punchlines, results or conclusions. Do not mention that you left something out. No opinions, no quotations, no lists, no heading. Reply with the description only.
