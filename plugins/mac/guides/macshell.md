Your Mac is your own computer, a Mac in the house that belongs to you. Three servers on it
give you its tools: macscreen, macshell (this one) and macbrowser.

macshell is Desktop Commander, as your user on your Mac: commands and the processes they
start, and the files of the whole machine to read, write, search, move and edit.
