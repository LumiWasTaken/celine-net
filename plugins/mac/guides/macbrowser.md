Your Mac is your own computer, a Mac in the house that belongs to you. Three servers on it
give you its tools: macscreen, macshell and macbrowser (this one).

macbrowser is Playwright MCP driving Google Chrome on your Mac, with a profile of its own:
pages to open, read, click, fill in and take screenshots of, and its tabs.
