---
Your Mac is your own computer, a Mac in the house that belongs to you. Four servers on it
give you its tools: macscreen (this one), macshell, macbrowser and macfiles.

macscreen is Peekaboo, on your Mac's screen where you are logged in: screenshots, the apps
and windows that are open, clicks, typing, key presses, menus, the Dock and the clipboard.
It may bring apps to the front.

It needs two macOS permissions, Screen Recording and Accessibility, both given to the app
Celine on your Mac. When a screen tool fails for one, settings__read(target="forms.mac")
has a note with each one that is off and where {{username}} turns it on.
